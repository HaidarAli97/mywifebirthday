FOR MY QUEEN — SEELAH

A simple, romantic birthday surprise in one page.

PREVIEW:
1. Open the folder.
2. Double-click index.html.
3. Tap the golden seal to open the envelope.

THINGS SHE CAN DO:
- Tap the candle to make a wish.
- Open the letter at the bottom.
- Play the soft melody from the button at the top right.

FILES:
- index.html: page content, envelope, candle and birthday letter
- style.css: design and responsive layout
- script.js: envelope, stars, petals, wish candle, music and letter reveal
- assets/wasila.jpg: add Wasila's photo here (optional)

PHOTO:
Her photo is saved as assets/wasila.jpg. Until it loads, an elegant "Seelah" monogram is shown instead.
A portrait photo looks best. To change it, replace assets/wasila.jpg with a new photo.

PUBLISH:
Upload the whole folder to any static hosting service, keeping assets beside index.html.
